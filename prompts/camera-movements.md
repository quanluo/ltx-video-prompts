# LTX Video Prompts: Camera Movements

Status: original, source-reviewed prompt drafts; not independently generated.

Use each prompt as a starting point. Keep the camera instruction consistent
with the subject motion and scene scale.

## 1. Slow dolly in

> A ceramic artist sits at a wheel shaping a tall vase. The clay rotates
> steadily while wet hands narrow the rim. The camera makes a slow, straight
> dolly toward the artist from a medium-wide shot to a close view of the hands.
> Soft north-window light reveals water and clay texture. The wheel hums and
> small drops strike the tray.

## 2. Lateral tracking shot

> A cyclist rides along a coastal road at sunrise. The camera tracks parallel
> at the cyclist's speed, keeping the rider centered while cliffs and ocean move
> behind in layered parallax. The jacket ripples in the wind, tires roll over
> fine gravel, and distant gulls call above the surf.

## 3. Clockwise orbit

> A glass sculpture stands on a dark pedestal in a quiet gallery. The camera
> performs one slow clockwise orbit at eye level. Reflections travel across the
> curved glass as the background columns shift behind it. Footsteps echo once
> in the distant room while the sculpture remains still.

## 4. Crane reveal

> A dense green hedge fills the frame. The camera rises smoothly above it,
> revealing a formal garden, a narrow reflecting pool, and an old stone house
> aligned in the distance. Morning mist drifts over the water as birds flutter
> from the trees and the garden fountain becomes audible.

## 5. Handheld follow

> A field researcher walks through tall grass toward a weather station during
> an approaching storm. A restrained handheld camera follows two steps behind,
> with natural footfall movement but no abrupt shake. Grass bends in gusts,
> instrument cables tap the mast, and low thunder rolls across the plain.
