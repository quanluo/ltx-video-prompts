# LTX Video Prompts: Product Video

Status: original, source-reviewed prompt drafts; not independently generated.

Replace generic objects with products you own or are authorized to advertise.
Do not imply capabilities, certifications, or results that the product lacks.

## 1. Mechanical keyboard

> A compact unbranded mechanical keyboard rests on a matte stone desk. The
> camera slides from left to right at key height as soft side light reveals the
> metal frame and textured keycaps. One hand presses three keys in sequence,
> producing distinct clean clicks. The final shot settles on the volume dial as
> it turns one notch.

## 2. Insulated bottle

> An unbranded stainless steel bottle stands on a wet rock beside a mountain
> stream. The camera begins on cold droplets running down the surface, tilts to
> the closed cap, then pulls back as a hiker picks it up. Water and fabric sounds
> remain natural. Avoid labels, text, or unsupported performance claims.

## 3. Desk lamp

> A minimal black desk lamp is assembled on a pale oak desk. In one continuous
> shot, a hand rotates the base, raises the arm, and taps the power control. The
> warm beam spreads across a notebook while the rest of the room remains cool.
> The camera makes a small arc to show the hinge movement and stable base.

## 4. Running shoe

> An unbranded running shoe sits on a turntable against a neutral gray studio
> background. The camera holds a close three-quarter view while the shoe rotates
> slowly. A narrow light travels across the mesh, midsole, and tread. The shoe
> then lands naturally on a clean track surface, with one soft impact and no
> exaggerated technical claims.

## 5. Portable speaker

> A small unbranded portable speaker stands on a picnic table at dusk. The
> camera pushes in as a hand presses the power button and a subtle indicator
> light appears. Nearby leaves move to a gentle rhythm while friends remain
> softly out of focus. Keep the product sharp and avoid rendering logos or
> interface text.
