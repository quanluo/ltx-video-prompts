# LTX Video Prompts: Cinematic Scenes

Status: original, source-reviewed prompt drafts; not independently generated.

## 1. Rainy night workshop

> A watchmaker works alone at a narrow bench after midnight, adjusting a tiny
> brass gear beneath a magnifying lens. Rain runs down the window behind him.
> The camera begins on the ticking mechanism, then racks focus to his tired
> eyes. One warm desk lamp shapes the scene against cool blue street light.
> Metal tools click softly while rain and a distant tram fill the room.

## 2. Desert radio tower

> At sunset, a technician climbs the final ladder of a remote radio tower above
> a vast desert. The camera holds a wide profile as the figure reaches the
> platform and connects a cable. Orange light fades into violet sky, warning
> lamps blink on, wind pulls at loose fabric, and the antenna begins a low
> electrical hum.

## 3. Empty theater

> A stage manager crosses an empty theater carrying a single work light. The
> camera moves backward down the center aisle as the light sweeps over rows of
> red seats. Dust floats in the beam, curtains breathe in a draft, and the
> manager's footsteps echo until the stage light clicks on.

## 4. Winter observatory

> Snow moves sideways around a mountain observatory at dawn. Inside the open
> dome, an astronomer turns a large control wheel and the telescope slowly
> rotates toward a pale horizon. The shot starts outside and passes through the
> doorway into warmer light. Motors whine, ice cracks underfoot, and wind
> softens as the camera enters.

## 5. Flooded library

> An abandoned library is covered by ankle-deep clear water. A conservator in
> rubber boots carries a sealed archival box between tall shelves. The camera
> glides just above the water, capturing rippling reflections of green emergency
> lights. Each careful step sends rings across the floor while loose pages rustle
> in a weak current.
