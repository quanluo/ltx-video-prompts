# LTX Video Prompts: Character Dialogue

Status: original, source-reviewed prompt drafts; not independently generated.

Use fictional people and invented dialogue. Obtain consent before conditioning
on a real person's face or voice.

## 1. Calm instruction

> A middle-aged marine biologist stands beside a tide-pool display in a quiet
> laboratory, facing the camera in a medium shot. She lifts a sample jar, pauses,
> and says in a calm clear voice, "The color change tells us the water is
> recovering." She lowers the jar and looks toward the tank. Pumps bubble softly
> in the background while cool overhead light reflects from the glass.

## 2. Workshop discovery

> A young bicycle mechanic crouches beside an open gear hub, then looks up at a
> coworker outside the frame. With surprised relief he says, "I found it. The
> spring was installed backward." He holds up the small spring, laughs once, and
> returns to the wheel. The camera remains static and tool sounds continue behind
> him.

## 3. Quiet disagreement

> Two fictional architects stand over a paper model in a sunlit studio. The
> first points to the central courtyard and says, "If we close this side, the
> morning light disappears." The second studies the model, moves one wall, and
> replies, "Then we open the roof instead." Their voices are measured, with
> short natural pauses and faint city noise through the windows.

## 4. Field report

> A weather reporter stands under a shelter as heavy rain crosses an empty road.
> Looking directly at the camera, she says, "The river is rising, but this bridge
> remains open for emergency vehicles only." A gust moves her jacket and she
> waits as thunder interrupts, then adds, "Residents should use the northern
> route." Rain remains louder than the distant traffic.

## 5. Museum greeting

> An elderly fictional curator opens a wooden display case and turns toward a
> small tour group. Smiling gently, he says, "This map was drawn by someone who
> had never seen the sea." He lets the sentence settle, unfolds one corner of
> the map, and adds, "That is why the coastline looks like a memory." The room
> is quiet except for paper movement and a wall clock.
