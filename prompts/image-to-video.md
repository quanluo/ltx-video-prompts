# LTX Video Prompts: Image-to-Video

Status: original, source-reviewed prompt drafts; not independently generated.

For image-conditioned generation, describe motion that is plausible from the
reference image. Avoid restating every visible object.

## 1. Portrait with environmental motion

> The subject remains seated and turns slightly toward the window. A light
> breeze moves loose hair and the edge of the curtain. Sunlight shifts gently
> across the face while the camera performs a very slow push in. Preserve the
> subject's clothing, facial structure, and room layout.

## 2. Landscape depth

> Mist moves slowly through the valley from left to right while foreground grass
> bends in separate small gusts. The camera advances along the existing path,
> creating natural parallax between rocks, trees, and distant mountains.
> Preserve the original season, weather, and terrain.

## 3. Product turn

> The product rotates twenty degrees clockwise on the existing surface and then
> stops. Reflections move consistently across the material while the camera
> remains locked. Preserve proportions, color, background, and all physical
> details; do not invent logos or text.

## 4. Architectural reveal

> The camera tilts upward from the entrance to the roofline while people in the
> distance walk at normal speed. Tree shadows move lightly across the facade.
> Preserve straight architectural lines, window spacing, material texture, and
> the original lighting direction.

## 5. Food steam and service

> Steam rises irregularly from the dish while a hand enters from the right and
> places a small garnish at the center. The camera makes a subtle forward move.
> Preserve the plate design, ingredient arrangement, table surface, and natural
> food texture.
