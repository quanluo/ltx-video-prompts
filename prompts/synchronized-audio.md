# LTX Video Prompts: Synchronized Audio

Status: original, source-reviewed prompt drafts; not independently generated.

Write sound events in the same chronological order as their visible causes.

## 1. Train platform

> An empty rural platform sits under light rain. A signal changes from red to
> green with a mechanical click. Two seconds later, a train enters from the left;
> wheel noise grows, brakes squeal briefly, and rain becomes quieter as the first
> carriage blocks the wind. The camera remains beneath the station roof.

## 2. Coffee preparation

> A barista locks a portafilter into an espresso machine. Metal contacts metal
> with a short click, the pump starts, and coffee flows into a ceramic cup. Steam
> hisses once as milk is heated. The camera moves from the handle to the cup in a
> single close tracking shot, matching each sound to the visible action.

## 3. Forest footbridge

> A hiker crosses a narrow wooden bridge after rain. Each boot produces a
> separate damp knock, boards flex slightly, and water runs beneath at a steady
> volume. Halfway across, a bird calls from the right and the hiker turns toward
> the sound. The camera follows from behind at walking speed.

## 4. Pottery kiln

> A potter opens a small kiln after it has cooled. The latch scrapes, the heavy
> door creaks, and loose ceramic pieces settle with tiny taps. The potter removes
> one glazed bowl and places it on a wooden table with a soft solid contact. Room
> tone remains quiet and no music plays.

## 5. Harbor rope

> A deckhand pulls a thick rope around a metal cleat as a small boat moves
> against the dock. Rope fibers drag audibly, the cleat rings once under tension,
> and water slaps the hull in irregular beats. The camera stays close to the
> hands, then pans to the boat as the line becomes taut.
